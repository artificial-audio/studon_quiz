# Numerical Precision Calculation

## Quiz

Calculate the value of the following expression, rounding to four decimal places:

$$
\frac{e^{0.5} \cdot \ln(10)}{2\pi}
$$

Where $e \approx 2.71828$ and $\pi \approx 3.14159$.

## Answer

- Correct Answer: 0.6024
- Tolerance: 0.01
- Points: 3
- Max Characters: 10
